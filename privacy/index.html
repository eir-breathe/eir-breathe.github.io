Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Eir is a guided breathing app. It is designed to work entirely on your iPhone. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your sessions. Completed breathing sessions (date, duration, cycles, and the rhythm you chose) are stored locally on your iPhone to show your progress.
  • Your preferences. Settings such as haptic and voice guidance, weekly goal, and reminder time are stored locally on your iPhone.

This data never leaves your device. Eir has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple Health

If you turn it on, Eir saves completed sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. Eir only writes this data and never reads your Health data. Data written to Health is managed by Apple and stays under your control in the Health app. Eir never uses Health data for advertising or shares it with anyone. You can turn this off at any time in Eir’s settings or in the Health app.

Notifications

If you enable a practice reminder, Eir schedules a local notification on your iPhone. No information is sent anywhere.

Siri, Shortcuts, widgets, and Live Activities

These features run through iOS on your device. Eir does not receive or send any data through them.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply to you. We do not add you to any list.

Children

Eir does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
