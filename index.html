Support

Questions, feedback, or a problem with the app? Write to eir.breathe.app@gmail.com.

Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Eir has no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete a session?

In Progress, tap a day you practiced, then swipe left on the session.

How do I save sessions to Apple Health?

Open Settings from Progress and turn on Apple Health. Sessions are saved as Mindful Minutes.

Note

Eir is a wellness app, not a medical device. If you feel lightheaded, return to your natural breath.
